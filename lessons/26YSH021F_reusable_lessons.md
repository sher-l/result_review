# 26YSH021F reusable lessons

- Gene-set traceability: report-declared gene-set file and final N must be checked against delivered files and unique counts.
- Validation leakage: external validation cohorts must not be used for model selection or optimized cutoff grouping.
- Scope consistency: MSS/mitochondrial study claims must be downgraded when spatial or validation datasets are ordinary CRC or when the mitochondrial branch was removed.
- Figure audit: visual readability does not close figure numbering, caption, or cross-reference defects.
